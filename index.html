07After darkSeven settings from my own tool, running live

Solar
interference

Your feed was full of eclipse photos. Here’s one that became a screen.

The moon works like a dimmer on the sun: bright disk to thin sliver. Line those phases up in a grid and each one becomes a pixel.

Light is both a particle and a wave, so I let a field of suns ripple like one. Every pixel is the same eclipse, caught in a different moment.
